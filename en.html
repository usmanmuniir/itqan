Services

From one replacement door to a full kitchen

We won't push you into a new kitchen if the one you have is sound. We inspect it and tell you honestly whether a refit is enough.

01

Aluminium kitchens

The most requested option in Madinah. It doesn't warp or rot in heat and humidity, and outlasts wood in this climate.

02

Wood and natural veneer

High density board or oak and walnut veneer, with sealed edges that keep moisture out of the panel.

03

Acrylic and poly-lacquer

Gloss or matt fronts in a colour you choose. Scratch resistant and cleans with a single wipe.

04

Kitchen renovation

New fronts, hinges, drawers and worktop while keeping the carcass. Saves a large share of the cost of a full replacement.

05

Quartz, marble and Corian worktops

Cut and fitted with as few joins as possible, with an integrated sink and the edge profile you want.

06

Repairs and modifications

Door replacement, hinge and drawer repair, extra units, and removal and refitting when you move house.

FAQ

What clients ask us

How long does a new kitchen take?

Two to four weeks depending on size and material. A renovation is faster and usually finishes within a week.

How much does a kitchen cost in Madinah?

Pricing is per linear metre and varies with the material, worktop type and number of units. After the measure visit you get a written, itemised quote with no obligation.

Can a kitchen be renovated without removing it completely?

Often yes. In many cases the carcass is sound and only the fronts, hinges and worktop need replacing. We tell you honestly after inspecting it.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, a written warranty covering fabrication and fitting. We explain its length and what it covers before you sign anything.

Do you work outside Al Madinah?

Our main work is inside Al Madinah and its districts. For larger projects elsewhere, contact us and we'll see whether we can take it on.
